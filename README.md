### Featured Work

<details open>
  <summary><b>Anatomy-TTA</b> — <code>In Active Research</code> · <i>Medical Computer Vision &amp; Ultrasound CAD</i></summary>
  <br>
  <blockquote>
    A test-time adaptation and segmentation framework designed to address cross-center acoustic distribution shifts and performance degradation in clinical thyroid ultrasound.
  </blockquote>
  <ul>
    <li><b>Methodological Core</b>: Dual-branch topology combining thyroid gland localization with nodule segmentation.</li>
    <li><b>Anatomical Containment Loss</b>: Penalizes anatomically impossible predictions that fall outside gland boundaries.</li>
    <li><b>Dynamic Adaptation</b>: Updates normalization parameters at test-time on unlabelled target scans without retraining.</li>
    <li><b>Status</b>: Active MSCS graduate research immersion and empirical multi-center benchmarking.</li>
  </ul>
</details>
